# Corporate Threat Assessment & Management

**Blue Line Intelligence Research — Practitioner Series**  
**Publication:** BLI-PR-001 | **Version:** 1.7 | **November 2026**

An independent practitioner guide to receiving and assessing workplace concerns, validating information, developing competing hypotheses, documenting analytical confidence, and recommending proportionate management responses. Includes a practical 12-step workflow.

## Audience and limitations

For corporate security, investigations, protective intelligence, and threat-management practitioners. This is an educational resource, not a validated assessment instrument, legal or clinical advice, or an ATAP/FBI-endorsed publication. Use organizational policies, applicable law, and qualified consultation for actual cases.
