# A Practitioner’s Guide to Digital Evidence Preservation

**Blue Line Intelligence Research — Practical Intelligence Methods**  
**Publication:** BLI-PR-006  
**Version:** 1.0 | November 2026

A field-oriented guide to preserving digital evidence with documented provenance, integrity checks, chain of custody, context, and responsible handoff. Includes an evidence register, preservation checklist, and fictional exercise.

## Topics

Authority and scope; triage; provenance; original and working copies; hashes; custody; timestamps; screenshots and exports; quality review.

**Use:** Independent practitioner education. Not a substitute for legal authorization, validated forensic procedures, or technical examination.
