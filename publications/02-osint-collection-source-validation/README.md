# BLI-PR-002 — OSINT Collection & Source Validation

**Blue Line Intelligence Research | Practical Intelligence Methods**  
**Version 1.0 | November 2026**

*A Practitioner’s Guide to OSINT Collection & Source Validation* provides a practical workflow for collecting publicly available information, assessing provenance and reliability, avoiding false corroboration, documenting uncertainty, and producing decision-ready intelligence.

## Inside the paper

- Scope, authority, and proportionate collection
- Collection logs, source registers, and preservation considerations
- Account attribution and source reliability
- Independent corroboration, timelines, and alternative hypotheses
- Fictional worked scenario, reporting model, and reusable worksheets
