# A Practitioner’s Guide to Cyber Threat Intelligence

**Blue Line Intelligence Research — Practitioner Series**  
**Publication:** BLI-PR-007  
**Version:** 1.0 | November 2026

A practical, independent guide to turning cyber observations into decision-ready intelligence. Covers intelligence requirements, CTI layers, source validation, behavioral analysis, uncertainty, and proportionate response, with a fictional phishing case and reusable briefing template.
